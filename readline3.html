    KLM to receive its first Airbus A350 900



    KLM Royal Dutch Airlines is preparing to take delivery of its first ever Airbus A350 900 later this month.


    The arrival of the jet is the first of 22 Airbus A350 900s set to fly for the dutch carrier on long-haul routes replacing older jets such as the Airbus A330 and Boeing 777(specifically the -200 variant).




    KLM A350 will feature a four-class cabin designed around premium demand






    According to aeroLOPA, the KLM Airbus A350-900 aircraft will feature:


    34 World Business class seats

    26 Premium seats

    271 Economy seats (with 33 Economy Comfort seats)


    KLM A350 World Business Class introduces STELIA Aerospace OPERA suites







    In business class, KLM has selected the OPERA model, designed and manufactured by STELIA Aerospace. These are the same business class suites that KLM partner Air France selected for its Airbus A350s.
